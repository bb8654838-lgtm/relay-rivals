    FIELD MANUAL
    Win the relay in 6 rounds
    1. One action per turn. Focus for +2 energy, deploy a unit for 1 energy, fortify a controlled node, or march to a neighboring node.
    2. Capture territory. A stronger march takes the target. Shields absorb one incoming unit before combat.
    3. Score every round. Each controlled node earns one point. The starting player rotates, so tempo matters.
    4. Highest score wins. Ties break by territory, then units, energy, and finally player order — there is always one winner.
